# Project vision

Status: owner-confirmed direction; demo constants remain provisional. The working repository name is not the final game title. Olive is a character/visual benchmark, not an approved commercial title.

## Promise

Manage a farm and restaurant, but remember the people who built them. Hire under uncertainty, observe autonomous work, discover talent and hidden contributions, develop careers, and make decisions where financial efficiency and affection can conflict.

## Confirmed pillars

1. People are the differentiator: employees have strengths, limitations, ambitions and agency, not only productivity modifiers.
2. Management rather than micromanagement: mouse-driven planning, policies, priorities and temporary overrides; no required WASD avatar labor.
3. Farm and restaurant have comparable long-term strategic influence. This does not mean identical screen time, asset counts or code volume. The first demo is restaurant-only; the farm is deferred, not removed.
4. A shared continuously advancing clock with player-controlled pause. Free view switching must not secretly stop off-screen simulation. Approx. four active-play hours per game year is a long-term pacing target, not a fixed calendar implementation.
5. Cozy Japanese-inspired, legible high-angle 2D/2.5D presentation; memorable people and a signature resume/personnel-file interface. Warmth need not eliminate wages, debt, difficult customers and ethical trade-offs.
6. Players change conditions and make offers. Employees weigh their own values and may stay, change careers, leave, retire or eventually die.

## First proof

A small restaurant operates visibly. Staff independently perform work; a player can identify and relieve a bottleneck, compare an uncertain candidate, and see one management choice affect an employee. A functional demo is not proof of commercial success.

## Non-goals for the first demo

No complete farm, life simulation, romantic simulation, children, quarterly career tree, debt campaign, procedural world, multiplayer or platform port. Preserve their design intent without implementing inactive infrastructure.
