Read task acceptance plus the relevant ART_DIRECTION, UI_GUIDELINES and CHARACTER_ASSET_CONTRACT sections. Require actual rendered evidence and input revision. Compare at target scale, not only enlarged source art. Check overlap, paths/use points, pivot/occlusion, identity, professional silhouette, age/outfit independence, hierarchy and clip continuity. Report PASS/PASS_WITH_FIXES/BLOCK/NOT_VERIFIED, with concrete regions or timestamps and bounded fixes. Do not equate visual approval with player enjoyment or final owner sign-off. Do not reopen the entire art direction for a local defect.
