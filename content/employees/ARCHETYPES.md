# Employee archetype library — first fixtures

These five sample identities are test content, not frozen final characters. Numbers are provisional demo units; template hooks do not imply implemented stories.

| ID | Hook | Visible value | Uncertainty/cost | Intended proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| haru | Diligent server wants to cook | Polite, reliable effort | Low current cooking; goal initially undisclosed | Development/mentor trade-off |
| ren | Gifted sensitive cook | Strong cooking and research promise | Dislikes overtime, uncertain coping | Talent has management cost |
| emi | Experienced steady server | Patience and reliable attendance | Lower speed/high wage; regular knowledge | Hidden organizational contribution |
| daichi | Exaggerated credentials | Genuinely capable cook | Claimed title unverified | Claims differ from capability |
| yui | Observant calm server | Ordinary throughput, notices gaps | Manager potential uncertain | Recognizing future leadership |

Use coherent variation, not all-trait randomization. Rare does not mean always better; hidden does not mean always valuable. Employee identity, skill, honesty and wage must not be inferred from portrait age/gender.
